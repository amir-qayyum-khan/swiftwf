SwiftTales Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

1. About us

SwiftTales Studio publishes animated videos on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

2. This website

This website does not collect any personal data itself. It has no accounts, forms, advertising or tracking cookies.

3. The SwiftTales Publisher app

SwiftTales Publisher is a private tool operated by SwiftTales Studio. It uses Google / YouTube API Services and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) APIs only to upload and manage the owner's own content: videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and posts on channels and pages owned by SwiftTales Studio. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from viewers or other users.

4. Data use, storage and sharing

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not share data with third parties, except sending our own content to YouTube, Facebook and Instagram through their official APIs.
  • Authorization tokens are stored only as needed to operate the tool, in access-restricted systems, and are deleted when no longer needed or when access is revoked.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

SwiftTales Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

See also the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

6. Meta platforms

Use of Facebook and Instagram is also governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

7. Revoking access

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook Settings → Business Integrations → remove SwiftTales Publisher.

You can also email us to request deletion of any stored data.
